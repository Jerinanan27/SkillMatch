# SkillMatch

**A platform connecting university students with freelance micro-projects.**

SkillMatch is a product and project-management case study (CSE495) for a
two-sided marketplace: it gives university students real-world project
experience and income, while giving startups and small businesses affordable,
skilled help for short-term tasks. This repository contains the full planning
package — discovery, scope, roadmap, and costing.

## Problem, Market & Solution

- **Problem:** students struggle to gain real-world experience during university.
- **Market need:** startups and small businesses need affordable, skilled help
  for short-term tasks.
- **Gap:** no dedicated platform connects students with freelance micro-jobs.
- **Solution:** SkillMatch matches student skills to real freelance projects.

## Objectives (SMART)

- **Specific** — connect students with freelance micro-jobs (registration & skill
  profiling, job browsing & filtering, application/proposal submission, built-in
  messaging).
- **Measurable** — 50+ student profiles and 20+ job postings in the pilot.
- **Achievable** — run the pilot using university resources.
- **Relevant** — serve both students and startups.
- **Time-Bound** — deliver an MVP within five months.

## MVP Scope & Pilot Success Metrics

- **Adoption:** 50+ students with complete profiles; 20+ freelance job postings.
- **Engagement:** 10+ successful matches (student hired, project completed, feedback given).
- **Satisfaction:** 80%+ overall satisfaction from students and clients.

## Project Management Artifacts

The planning follows a standard PM structure:

- **Project Charter** — purpose, scope, and budget estimate (BDT).
- **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)** — Level 0 to Level 2 task decomposition.
- **Network Diagram** — task sequencing and the critical path.
- **Gantt Chart** — timeline of roughly 147 days (~4.9 months).
- **Costing** — a 10-component cost framework.
